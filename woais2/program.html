Program (Preliminary)

Workshop date: December 14, 2026 (Monday) — Middleware 2026 Workshops Day 1

Workshop hours: 8:30–16:30 (CET), across the four workshop session slots

Location: Universitat Rovira i Virgili, Tarragona, Spain (hybrid — in-person and remote participants); room to be announced by the Middleware organizers

Hosted by: 27th ACM/IFIP International Middleware Conference (December 14–18, 2026) — see the full conference program

Accepted papers: 12, presented across four sessions, plus two keynotes

This program is preliminary. Session times follow the Middleware 2026 workshop slots and are not expected to change, but the order of talks may still be adjusted. Keynote speakers are to be announced, and the presenter shown for each paper is provisional (listed as the first author until confirmed with the authors). Registration, coffee breaks and lunch are shared with the other Middleware workshops.
